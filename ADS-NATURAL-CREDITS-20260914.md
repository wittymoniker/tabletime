# Tabletime natural ad credits — 2026-09-14

- Paid ad-credit purchases are disabled.
- 1 ad credit = 1 counted impression.
- A boosted impression spends exactly 1 credit from the advertiser's allocated boost budget.
- When that impression is counted on another user's profile/home ad space, that page owner earns exactly 1 credit.
- Self-placement does not return the spent credit to the advertiser.
- Existing 10-minute unique-impression deduplication remains in place.
- Private/public/global targeting and the ad post subtype remain available.
- Legacy Stripe/ad-revenue database fields are left in place only for backward compatibility; this build does not use them for ad credits.
